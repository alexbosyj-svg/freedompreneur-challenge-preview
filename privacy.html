Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

Remote Leads OU ("Freedompreneur", "we", "us", or "our") operates the Freedompreneur website and associated services (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, share, retain and delete information when you use the Site, and the rights and choices you have.

By accessing or using the Site, you agree to the practices described below. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Data

Information you provide directly when purchasing our products or contacting us:

  • Name and email address
  • Billing details (processed by our payment provider — we never see or store your full card number)
  • Purchase history, including any add-ons or upgrades you choose
  • Messages you send us and questions you ask in live sessions
  • Information about your business that you choose to share with us

1.2 Usage Data

Automatically collected technical data:

  • IP address and approximate location
  • Browser and device type
  • Pages you visit and how long you stay
  • The ad or link that brought you to the Site

1.3 Cookies & Similar Technologies

We use cookies to:

  • Keep the Site and checkout working properly
  • Understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve it
  • Measure our ads (for example Meta Pixel) and show relevant ads

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the Site may not work without them.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Process your order and give you access to what you bought
  • Send you challenge details, session links and replays
  • Answer your questions and give support
  • Manage subscriptions and trials you sign up for, including renewals
  • Send you marketing emails, which you can unsubscribe from at any time
  • Improve the Site, our programs and our ads
  • Prevent fraud and meet our legal obligations

Legal bases under GDPR/UK-GDPR: We process your data to perform our contract with you (your purchase), on the basis of our legitimate interests (improving and promoting our services), with your consent (marketing cookies and emails where required), and to meet legal obligations (tax and accounting).

4. Information Sharing

We never sell or rent your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Service providers: Companies that help us run payments, email, hosting and video sessions.
  • Advertising partners: Platforms like Meta that help us measure and show our ads.
  • Legal requirements: When the law or a court requires it.
  • Business transfers: If our business is sold or merged, your data moves with it under this same policy.

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Access and Control

You have the right to:

  • See the data we hold about you
  • Fix anything that's wrong
  • Ask us to delete your data
  • Get a copy of your data
  • Object to or limit how we use it
  • Withdraw your consent at any time

6.2 Marketing Communications

Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link. Click it and we'll stop. We'll still send emails about things you bought, like session links and receipts.

6.3 Do Not Track

Our Site does not currently respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

7. International Transfers

We are based in Estonia (EU). Some of our service providers are in the United States. When your data leaves the EU, we rely on safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures including:

  • Encrypted (HTTPS) connections on every page
  • Payments handled by a PCI-compliant provider
  • Access to data limited to people who need it
  • Strong passwords and two-step login on our tools
  • Regular review of the tools we use

No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

9. Children's Privacy

The Site is for adults running a business. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think we have, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. California Privacy Rights

California residents have additional rights under the CCPA including:

  • Know what personal data we collect and why
  • Ask us to delete it
  • Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your data (we don't sell it)
  • Correct data that's wrong
  • Not be treated differently for using these rights

11. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top. For big changes we'll also let you know by email.
